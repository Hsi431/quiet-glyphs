繁體中文

「安靜的字:錦鯉」(Quiet Glyphs: Koi)是一款 Wear OS 錶面。

  • 我們不收集任何資料。錶面不收集、不儲存、不傳送任何個人資料或使用資料。
  • 不連網。錶面不連接網際網路，也不會把資訊送出你的手錶。
  • 不要求任何權限。錶面不存取聯絡人、相片、位置、麥克風、相機或其他個人資料。
  • 沒有廣告、沒有追蹤、沒有第三方分析工具。
  • 錶面會讀取手錶上的時間、日期與電量，只用來顯示時間、日期及電量圈，資料只在手錶上使用。
  • 你選擇的時間字型、魚的花色與睡蓮設定只保存在手錶上。
  • 購買由 Google Play 處理，我們不會取得你的付款資訊。

若本政策日後有變更，會更新本頁並修改上方的生效日期。

English

Quiet Glyphs: Koi is a Wear OS watch face.

  • We collect no data. The watch face does not collect, store, or transmit personal or usage data.
  • No internet connection. The watch face does not connect to the internet or send information off your watch.
  • No permissions requested. It does not access your contacts, photos, location, microphone, camera, or other personal data.
  • No ads, tracking, or third-party analytics.
  • The watch face reads the watch's time, date, and battery level only to display the clock, date, and battery ring. This information is used on the watch.
  • Your choices for time font, koi colors, and lily pads are stored on the watch.
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play; we never receive your payment details.

If this policy changes, this page will be updated along with the effective date above.
